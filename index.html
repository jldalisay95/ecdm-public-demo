    Policy-to-field traceability

    Policy basis

    Coverage and authorization

    Coverage must be confirmed under RA 7305 and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations. Required determinations and approvals remain with authorized officials.

    Republic Act No. 7305

    Exposure threshold

    The model documents qualifying exposure for at least 50% of authorized working hours. It does not measure clinical dose, severity, or probability.

    Revised implementing rules

    Current rate schedule

    The salary-grade rate is an exact lookup from DBM–DOH Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2016. The application does not interpolate or create new rates.

    DBM–DOH Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2016

    Judicial interpretation

    The application excludes the invalidated 2012 high/low exposure-day rate matrix. A recorded high/low label does not change the 2016 salary-grade rate.

    Cawad v. Abad, G.R. No. 207145
    Policy review date: 21 August 2026. Later laws, circulars, jurisprudence, or authorized interpretations may require the model to be revised.

    Public demonstration boundaries

    About this prototype

    What it demonstrates

    • Task-level exposure documentation
    • Unique-hours calculation and overlap flags
    • Coverage, exclusion, threshold, and approval gates
    • Official salary-grade rate lookup
    • Explainable status and printable summary

    What it does not do

    • Determine legal entitlement
    • Approve hazard allowance or payment
    • Replace agency, LCE, DOH, budget, accounting, or audit review
    • Store or transmit an assessment
    • Connect to HR or payroll systems

    Data handling

    The application has no database, sign-in, cookies, analytics, uploads, or browser-storage code. Inputs exist only in the open page and are lost when the page is refreshed or closed. Hosting providers may retain ordinary access or security logs.

    Institutional adaptation

    An institution should first inventory its existing authorized procedure. Use of real employee records would require a separate privacy, security, legal, records-management, procurement, and institutional-governance assessment.
